A call has one price. It is set by the model's owner, paid by the caller, and split at settlement between the owner, the operator and the protocol.

## The price

The owner sets a price, its pricing unit (`call` or `token`), and the mint it is paid in: SOL, or any SPL Token mint. `sketch-32` and `text-1m` are priced in SOL.

- **`call` (`PriceUnit::Call`).** One flat price per call. Opening a call via `open_call` deposits `model.price` into escrow.
- **`token` (`PriceUnit::Token`).** A unit price per output token. Opening a call via `open_metered_call` deposits a caller-chosen escrow `ceiling` (at least `model.price`, and above `0` whenever `model.price > 0`) that caps the caller's maximum spend.

The price is an integer in the mint's base units (lamports for SOL). The owner changes the price, mint, operator share or `price_unit` with `set_price`. A call locks the price, unit and ceiling in force when it opened.

## Escrow

Opening a call moves the escrow amount (`model.price` for a per-call model, or `ceiling` for a per-token model) from the caller into the call's escrow. It stays there until one of two things happens:

- the call settles with the enclave's signature: `settle_call` pays out `model.price`, or `settle_metered_call` pays out `count * price` (`<= ceiling`) across owner, operator and protocol while returning `ceiling - total` (`returned_amount`) to the caller in the same instruction (`count = 0` returns 100% of `ceiling`)
- the deadline passes, and the whole escrow (including the full `ceiling` on a per-token call) returns to the caller

There is no third way out of escrow.

## The split

Settlement pays three parties in the instruction that writes the receipt:

| Party | Receives |
| --- | --- |
| Operator | `price × operator share / 10,000`, rounded down |
| Protocol | `price × protocol share / 10,000`, rounded down |
| Owner | the rest of the price |

Shares are in basis points: 10,000 is the whole price.

- **The operator share** is set by the owner for each model, with the price. It is the maximum share an operator earns for answering a call to that model. An enclave operator can quote a lower share on their `Grant` via `set_quote` (`quoted_operator_share_bps <= model.operator_share_bps`); calls opened on that grant snapshot the quoted share and the model owner keeps the difference.
- **The protocol share** is one rate for every model. It is `protocolShareBps` in `GET /v1/config`.
- The operator share plus the protocol share is at most 10,000.

The owner, the operator, the fee recipient and both shares are fixed in the call when it opens, so a call settles on the terms it opened with. The three amounts are written into the receipt.

When a model owner attaches an on-chain [`Split`](/docs/program/accounts#split) PDA (`set_split`, up to 8 recipients summing to 10,000 basis points), enclaves automatically attach the `Split` PDA during `settle_call` and `settle_metered_call` so `owner_amount` routes into the `Split` PDA instead of directly to `owner`, crediting each recipient's `accrued` share (`floor(owner_amount * share_bps / 10,000)` with any rounding dust to `recipients[0]`). Any wallet can then call `claim_split` (or press **Claim** under **Revenue split** on `/models/<name>`) to release a recipient's unclaimed balance directly to their wallet.

## What else a call costs

| Cost | Paid by |
| --- | --- |
| The transaction fee of `open_call` or `open_metered_call` | the caller |
| Rent for the Call account, 349 bytes plus 33 bytes per callback account | the caller |
| The transaction fee of `settle_call` or `settle_metered_call` | the settling payer (typically the operator's enclave) |
| Rent for the Receipt account, 407 bytes plus `open_answer` (423 bytes for metered calls) | the settling payer |

Sealing a model costs its owner the rent of the Model account and of the Weights accounts that hold the ciphertext, plus the fees of the upload's transactions.

## The token

<Rows data="token" />

VOYNICH is tied to calls. The protocol share of every settled call is paid to the launch wallet in the settling instruction. A crank buys VOYNICH on the market with what the wallet receives and burns what it bought.

Each receipt shows the protocol's amount and the wallet it went to.
