title: Grants

---

A grant is one model's key, wrapped so that one enclave alone can unwrap it. It is an account on chain. An enclave that holds a grant for a model can open that model, and nothing else can.

<Diagram name="grant" />

## The wrapped key

The model key is the 32 random bytes the weights were encrypted with. A grant holds it inside an envelope encrypted to the enclave's encryption key:

```text
0x01 (1 byte)
ephemeral X25519 public key of the sender (32 bytes)
XChaCha20 nonce (24 bytes)
the model key, encrypted, with its Poly1305 tag (32 + 16 bytes)
```

That is 105 bytes. The envelope's key is derived with X25519 and HKDF-SHA256 from the sender's ephemeral key and the enclave's encryption key, with the salt `sender public key, then recipient public key` and the info `voynich/grant/v1`.

The wrapped key is public, like the ciphertext. Only the enclave's encryption secret, held inside the enclave, unwraps it.

## A grant from the owner

The owner wraps the model key on their own device to an enclave and signs `grant_by_owner`. The program writes the grant when:

- the model is `sealed` or `live`
- the enclave's attestation record is in force at that slot
- the enclave's measurement is on the model's list of allowed measurements

The first grant moves the model from `sealed` to `live`.

## A grant from an enclave

An enclave that holds a grant can grant the next enclave. The owner does not sign and does not need to be online, so a model gains capacity as operators arrive.

The granting enclave reads the newcomer's Enclave account and attestation record, wraps the model key to the newcomer's encryption key inside the runtime, and signs `grant_by_enclave` with its signing key. The program writes the grant when:

- the model is `live`
- the granting enclave's own grant is not revoked
- both enclaves' attestation records are in force at that slot
- both measurements are on the model's list of allowed measurements

The key moves from an attested enclave to an attested enclave running software the owner allowed. The grant records which enclave made it.

## What an enclave does with a grant

When an enclave sees a grant made to it, it:

1. fetches the model's ciphertext from the Weights accounts
2. checks the SHA-256 of the ciphertext against the hash in the Model
3. unwraps the model key with its encryption secret
4. decrypts the weights and loads the model, all in the enclave's memory

From then it answers calls for that model.

## Revoking

The owner revokes a grant with `revoke_grant`. From that slot:

- no new call opens with that enclave for that model
- that enclave can no longer grant the model on
- calls already open with it settle or refund as usual

Granting the same enclave again replaces the grant and lifts the revocation.

A grant reads as `live`, `expired` while its enclave's attestation record is expired, or `revoked`.

## Where the key exists

<Diagram name="custody" />

The model key exists in the clear in two kinds of place:

- the owner's device, at sealing
- enclaves that hold an attestation record in force, with a measurement the owner listed

Everywhere else it exists wrapped: in Grant accounts on chain, in transit, in any copy anyone makes. The weights follow the same rule: ciphertext on chain and in transit, plaintext only in an enclave's memory.

Money follows the key. A call's escrow leaves only against a signature from such an enclave, or back to the caller. See [Calls and receipts](/docs/concepts/calls-and-receipts).

Granting and revoking step by step is in [Manage a model](/docs/guides/manage-a-model).
