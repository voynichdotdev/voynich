The `voynich` program is an Anchor program. Its address on a cluster is `programId` in `GET /v1/config`. Every account below starts with an 8-byte discriminator. All but Weights are Borsh-encoded after it. Integers are little-endian.

<Rows data="program-accounts" columns="account,discriminator,fields,bytes" />

`bytes` is the encoded length of an account whose fields are all fixed in size. Model, Enclave, Grant, Call and Receipt hold strings or lists, so their length varies inside the space they are created with.

| Account | Address | Space | Rent paid by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Config | PDA `["config"]` | 118 bytes (116 bytes before migration) | the admin |
| Model | PDA `["model", name]` | 8,192 bytes (6,000 bytes legacy) | the owner |
| Weights | any address, created by the owner | 56 bytes plus its ciphertext | the owner |
| Enclave | PDA `["enclave", signing_key]` | 470 bytes | the operator |
| Measurement | PDA `["measurement", digest[..32], digest[32..]]` | 366 bytes | the publisher |
| Grant | PDA `["grant", model, enclave]` | 223 bytes | the grant's payer |
| Call | PDA `["call", caller, nonce]` | 349 bytes plus 33 bytes per callback account (302, 303, 304 or 340 + 33 * N legacy) | the payer |
| Receipt | PDA `["receipt", call]` | 407 bytes plus `open_answer` (423 bytes for metered calls; 401 or 406 plus `open_answer` legacy) | the settling payer |

In a seed, `name` is the model's name as UTF-8, an address is its 32 bytes, and `nonce` is 8 bytes little-endian.

## Model

One per sealed model, at PDA `["model", name]`. Created by `create_model`. Changed by `attach_weights`, `seal_range`, `seal_model`, `set_price`, `set_allowed_measurements`, `retire_model`, `grant_by_owner` and `settle_call`.

<Rows data="account-model" columns="field,type,meaning" />

`status` is one byte: 0 `Uploading`, 1 `Sealed`, 2 `Live`, 3 `Retired`. `price_unit` is one byte: 0 `Call`, 1 `Token`. A measurement is 48 bytes. `ModelBase` is `sha256: [u8; 32]`, `size_bytes: u64`, `format: string` (`onnx`, `safetensors` or `gguf`, at most 32 bytes), and `source: string` (1 to 256 bytes starting with `https://`, `http://`, `ipfs://` or `hf://`, no ASCII whitespace or control bytes). Legacy 6,000-byte and earlier 8,192-byte Model accounts decode trailing zeros as `base: None` and `price_unit: Call`. What these facts mean is in [Sealed models](/docs/concepts/sealed-models).

## Weights

A Weights account holds ciphertext. It is not an Anchor-typed account: the program reads and writes it in place. It is a 56-byte header followed by the ciphertext bytes.

| Offset | Bytes | Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 8 | discriminator | Set by `create_model`. |
| 8 | 32 | `model` | The Model the account belongs to. |
| 40 | 4 | `index` | Its place in the model's `weights` list, from 0. |
| 44 | 4 | `size_bytes` | The ciphertext bytes it holds: its data length minus 56. |
| 48 | 4 | `written_bytes` | The highest end written so far. Equal to `size_bytes` once the account is written to its end. |
| 52 | 4 | `reserved` | Global ciphertext byte offset of this account (`0` for legacy models). |
| 56 | `size_bytes` | ciphertext | The model's ciphertext from this account's offset on. |

The owner creates each Weights account before attaching it: owned by the `voynich` program, rent-exempt, with its first 56 bytes zero, and between 57 bytes and 10 MiB long. A model has 1 to 64 of them (initial batch up to 8 in `create_model`, further accounts in `attach_weights`). The ciphertext of the model is the ciphertext of its Weights accounts in order, and their sizes add up to the Model's `size_bytes`.

## Measurement

One per published 48-byte SHA-384 enclave runtime measurement, at PDA `["measurement", digest[..32], digest[32..]]` (splitting the 48-byte digest across two seeds within Solana's 32-byte seed limit). Created by `publish_measurement` (`366` bytes). Marked `Deprecated` by `deprecate_measurement`.

<Rows data="account-measurement" columns="field,type,meaning" />

`status` is one byte: 0 `Active`, 1 `Deprecated`. `source_repo` is 1 to 128 bytes and `version` is 1 to 32 bytes (no ASCII whitespace or control characters); `commit_sha` is 7 to 64 hex characters; `digest` and `recipe_hash` are non-zero.

## Call

One per call, at PDA `["call", caller, nonce]`. Created by `open_call` or `open_metered_call`. Changed by `settle_call` and `refund_call`.

<Rows data="account-call" columns="field,type,offset,meaning" />

`status` is one byte: 0 `Open`, 1 `Settled`, 2 `Refunded`. `answer_mode` is one byte: 0 `Sealed`, 1 `Open`. `price_unit` is one byte: 0 `Call`, 1 `Token`. Each `CallbackAccount` is 33 bytes: `pubkey` (32 bytes) followed by `is_writable` (`bool`, 1 byte), up to 8 entries. `Call::escrow_amount()` returns `price` for `PriceUnit::Call` and `ceiling` for `PriceUnit::Token`.

The owner, operator, fee recipient, price, price unit, ceiling and both shares are copied into the Call when it opens. A price change, a new fee recipient or a new share after that leaves an open call as it was.

An escrow in SOL is held as lamports in the Call account itself, above its rent. An escrow in a mint is held in the Call's associated token account for that mint.

## Receipt

One per settled call, at PDA `["receipt", call]`. Created by `settle_call` (per-call) or `settle_metered_call` (per-token) and never changed.

<Rows data="account-receipt" columns="field,type,offset,meaning" />

`owner_amount`, `operator_amount` and `protocol_amount` add up to `total`. Flat per-call receipts stay at `407 + open_answer.len()` bytes. A metered receipt created by `settle_metered_call` writes the exact 407-byte `Receipt` prefix (`answer_mode = Sealed`, `open_answer = []`, `output_hash = running_hash`, `total = count * price`) followed by 16 trailing bytes (`Receipt::METERED_SPACE = 423`): `delivered_count: u64` (offset 407) and `returned_amount: u64` (offset 415). Opening a call via CPI and verifying a Receipt from your own program is in [Calling a model from a program](/docs/program/calling-a-model).
