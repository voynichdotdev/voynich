The program has 44 instructions.

<Rows data="program-instructions" columns="instruction,arguments,signers,does" />

Every instruction except `migrate_model` ends with two accounts that Anchor's event CPI needs: `event_authority`, the PDA `["__event_authority"]`, and `program`, the `voynich` program itself. Accounts are passed in the order of each table. An optional account that is left out is passed as the program's own address.

The errors named below are in [Errors](/docs/program/errors). The events are in [Events](/docs/program/events).

## initialize

Creates the Config. It can be sent once, by the program's upgrade authority.

<Rows data="ix-initialize-args" />

<Rows data="ix-initialize" />

- **Checks.** The signer is the program's upgrade authority (`Unauthorized`). The attestation program and the fee recipient are not the default address, the layout version is above 0, the protocol share is at most 10,000, the deadline is above 0 slots and `max_open_answer_bytes` is 1 to 4,096 (`InvalidConfig`).
- **Effects.** The Config holds the admin and the six values. Emits `ConfigChanged`.

## migrate_config

Reallocates a legacy 116-byte Config account to 118 bytes in place and writes `max_open_answer_bytes`.

<Rows data="ix-migrate-config-args" />

<Rows data="ix-migrate-config" />

- **Checks.** The signer is the Config's admin (`Unauthorized`). The Config is the PDA `["config"]`, owned by the program, 116 bytes long, and `max_open_answer_bytes` is 1 to 4,096 (`InvalidConfig`).

## migrate_model

Repairs trailing padding bytes in a pre-Build-4 Model account whose Borsh buffer shrunk during `seal_model` or `set_allowed_measurements`, so the trailing `price_unit` byte decodes cleanly as `PriceUnit::Call` (`0`).

<Rows data="ix-migrate-model" />

- **Checks.** `model` is owned by `voynich`, parses cleanly through `base`, and sits at PDA `["model", name]` with matching `bump` (`InvalidModel`). `authority` is either the model's `owner` or `config.admin` (`Unauthorized`).
- **Effects.** If the byte at `price_unit` is above `1` or any trailing padding byte after it is non-zero, zeroes the tail from `price_unit` onward and verifies `Model::try_deserialize` succeeds. Takes no arguments and emits no event.

## create_model

Creates a Model in `Uploading`, binds its Weights accounts to it and moves the upload funding to the upload key.

<Rows data="ix-create-model-args" />

<Rows data="ix-create-model" />

`manifest` is the manifest's compact UTF-8 JSON, as told in [The manifest on chain](/docs/concepts/sealed-models#on-chain). After the listed accounts, the transaction passes the model's initial Weights accounts in order, each writable, followed by `extra_upload_key_count` (`0` to `7`) additional writable non-signer upload keys as remaining accounts.

- **Checks.** The name is 1 to 32 bytes of lowercase letters and digits in groups joined by single hyphens; the manifest is 1 to 4,096 bytes; `size_bytes` is above 16; `cipher` is `xchacha20poly1305-64k-v1`; there are 1 to 8 allowed measurements; when `base` is `Some`, `sha256` is non-zero, `size_bytes` is above 0, `format` is `onnx`, `safetensors` or `gguf` (at most 32 bytes), and `source` is 1 to 256 bytes starting with `https://`, `http://`, `ipfs://` or `hf://` with no ASCII whitespace or control bytes; the operator share plus the protocol share is at most 10,000; `1 + extra_upload_key_count` is at most 8; the primary `upload_key` and every extra upload key are distinct from `owner` and each other, owned by the System program, hold no data and (for extra keys) are writable (`InvalidModel`). There is at least 1 Weights account (up to 64 total across `create_model` and `attach_weights`), each owned by the program, writable, rent-exempt, listed once, at most 10 MiB, with its first 56 bytes zero, and their cumulative ciphertext sizes do not exceed `size_bytes` (`InvalidWeights`).
- **Effects.** The Model exists with status `Uploading`, `hash_version = 1`, `sealed_chunks = 0`, empty `merkle_frontier`, `merkle_frontier_mask = 0`, `base` (when given), `price_unit` (`Call` or `Token`), and records `upload_key` and `upload_keys` (`[upload_key, ...extra_upload_keys]`). Each attached Weights account has its header initialized (with its global ciphertext byte offset in `reserved`). `upload_funding_lamports` is divided evenly across all `K = 1 + extra_upload_key_count` upload keys (remainder to `upload_key`) from `owner`. Emits `ModelCreated`.

## attach_weights

Binds additional preallocated Weights accounts to an uploading model (up to 64 total). Signed by any authorized upload key so attaching accounts beyond the initial `create_model` batch needs no extra owner signature.

<Rows data="ix-attach-weights" />

After the listed accounts, remaining accounts carry the last currently attached Weights account (read-only) followed by the new zero-header Weights accounts to bind in order, each writable.

- **Checks.** The signer is `model.upload_key` or an entry in `model.upload_keys` (`Unauthorized`). The model is `Uploading` (`ModelNotUploading`). The first remaining account is the model's last currently attached Weights account, each new account passes the same Weights checks as `create_model`, total Weights accounts stay at most 64, and cumulative ciphertext size stays at most `model.size_bytes` (`InvalidWeights`).
- **Effects.** Initializes each new Weights account header sequentially (writing its global byte offset to `reserved`), appends their keys to `model.weights`, and emits `WeightsAttached`.

## write_weights

Writes ciphertext bytes into one Weights account. Signed by any of the model's authorized upload keys alone, so an upload writes in parallel with no wallet prompt.

<Rows data="ix-write-weights-args" />

<Rows data="ix-write-weights" />

`offset` counts from the start of that account's ciphertext, not from the start of the model's.

- **Checks.** The signer is `model.upload_key` or an entry in `model.upload_keys` (`Unauthorized`). The model is `Uploading` (`ModelNotUploading`). The account is one of the model's Weights accounts (`InvalidWeights`). `bytes` is not empty and `offset` plus the length is at most the account's size (`InvalidWrite`).
- **Effects.** The bytes are in the account and `written_bytes` is the highest end written so far. Writes may land in any order. Writing a range again replaces it. Emits `WeightsWritten`.

## seal_range

Hashes 1 to 8 consecutive 65,552-byte ciphertext chunks (`CIPHER_CHUNK_BYTES`) starting at `model.sealed_chunks` into the model's binary Merkle frontier (`voynich/chunk/v1` leaves, `voynich/node/v1` internal nodes). Signed by any authorized upload key.

<Rows data="ix-seal-range-args" />

<Rows data="ix-seal-range" />

Remaining accounts carry the 1 or 2 Weights accounts covering the chunk byte range being hashed.

- **Checks.** The signer is an authorized upload key (`Unauthorized`). The model is `Uploading` (`ModelNotUploading`) with `hash_version == 1` (`InvalidModel`). `chunk_count` is 1 to 8 and does not exceed total chunks; the covering Weights accounts belong to the model, span the byte range cleanly, and are written to their end (`InvalidWeights`, `UploadIncomplete`).
- **Effects.** Updates `model.merkle_frontier`, `model.merkle_frontier_mask`, and increments `model.sealed_chunks` by `chunk_count`. Emits `RangeSealed`.

## seal_model

Finalizes the ciphertext root hash. From here the bytes cannot change.

<Rows data="ix-seal-model-args" />

<Rows data="ix-seal-model" />

For `hash_version == 0`, remaining accounts carry every Weights account of the model in order. For `hash_version == 1`, remaining accounts are empty when `seal_range` has already hashed every chunk, or carry the covering Weights accounts for up to 8 remaining chunks hashed inline.

- **Checks.** The signer is the model's owner (`Unauthorized`). The model is `Uploading` (`ModelNotUploading`). For `hash_version == 0`, every Weights account is written to its end (`UploadIncomplete`) and their flat SHA-256 equals `ciphertext_sha256` (`CiphertextHashMismatch`). For `hash_version == 1`, at most 8 chunks remain unhashed (`UploadIncomplete`), any remaining chunks hash cleanly, and folding `merkle_frontier` produces `ciphertext_sha256` (`CiphertextHashMismatch`).
- **Effects.** The Model holds the root hash and the sealing slot, its status is `Sealed`, `merkle_frontier` is cleared, `merkle_frontier_mask` is set to 0, `upload_key` is cleared to the default address and `upload_keys` is emptied, so nothing can write to its Weights accounts again. Emits `ModelSealed` (carrying `base_sha256: model.base.map(|b| b.sha256)`).

## open_call

Creates a per-call Call and moves the model's price into its escrow.

<Rows data="ix-open-call-args" />

<Rows data="ix-open-call" />

`nonce` is any number the caller has not used before: it gives the Call its address, `["call", caller, nonce]`. For a direct wallet call, `payer` and `caller` are the same signer. When another Solana program opens a call through CPI, `caller` is that program's system-owned PDA signed via `invoke_signed` (which holds the escrow and receives any refund), while `payer` is a signer paying the Call account rent and any escrow token account rent. `callback` is optional (`CallbackSpec { program: pubkey, accounts: Vec<CallbackAccount> }`, where each `CallbackAccount` is `{ pubkey: pubkey, is_writable: bool }`) and registers an `on_receipt` CPI target invoked during `settle_call`. For a price in SOL the five token accounts and programs are left out.

- **Checks.** The model is `Live` (`ModelNotLive`) and has `price_unit == PriceUnit::Call` (`InvalidCall`). The enclave's Grant for the model is not revoked (`GrantRequired`). The record is the one the Enclave names, valid and unexpired (`InvalidEnclave`, `InvalidAttestation`), and its measurement is allowed (`MeasurementNotAllowed`). When `model.has_policy` is true, remaining accounts must include the canonical `Policy` PDA (`PolicyRequired`) and, if `policy.allow_list_enabled` is true, `caller` must be in `policy.allowed_callers` (`PolicyCallerDenied`). The operator share plus the protocol share is at most 10,000 (`InvalidConfig`). When `callback` is `Some`: `callback.program` is neither the default address nor the `voynich` program itself, and `callback.accounts` has at most 8 entries (`InvalidCallback`). For a price in a mint: the mint is the model's price mint, the caller's token account holds that mint, and the escrow account is the Call's associated token account (`InvalidTokenAccount`).
- **Effects.** The Call holds the caller, the model, the enclave, the input hash, `answer_mode`, `caller_is_program` (`true` when invoked via CPI, `false` otherwise), `callback_program` and `callback_accounts` (default address and empty list when `callback` is `None`), `price_unit = Call`, `ceiling = model.price`, the price and mint, the owner, the operator, the fee recipient, `operator_share_bps` (snapshotted as `grant.quoted_operator_share_bps.map(|q| q.min(model.operator_share_bps)).unwrap_or(model.operator_share_bps)`), `protocol_share_bps`, the opening slot and the deadline slot (the opening slot plus the Config's `call_deadline_slots`). The price moves from `caller` into the escrow. Emits `CallOpened`.

## open_metered_call

Creates a per-token metered Call and moves the caller's escrow ceiling into its escrow. Shares the same `OpenCall` account layout as `open_call`, keeping `open_call`'s 4-argument ABI unchanged for existing CPI callers.

<Rows data="ix-open-metered-call-args" />

<Rows data="ix-open-metered-call" />

- **Checks.** The model is `Live` (`ModelNotLive`) and has `price_unit == PriceUnit::Token` (`InvalidCall`). `ceiling >= model.price`, and `ceiling > 0` whenever `model.price > 0` (`InvalidCall`). The enclave's Grant, attestation record, measurement, share sum, optional `callback`, and mint token accounts pass the same checks as `open_call`.
- **Effects.** Creates the Call with `price = model.price` (the per-token unit price), `price_unit = Token`, and `ceiling`, and deposits `ceiling` from `caller` into the Call's SOL or SPL escrow (`Call::escrow_amount()`). Emits `CallOpened` carrying `price_unit` and `ceiling`.

## settle_call

Checks the enclave's signature, writes the Receipt, invokes any registered `on_receipt` callback and pays out the escrow, in one instruction.

<Rows data="ix-settle-call-args" />

<Rows data="ix-settle-call" />

The transaction carries an Ed25519 program instruction before `settle_call`, and `ed25519_instruction_index` is its index. That instruction verifies exactly one signature and carries the signature, the enclave's signing key and the [receipt message](/docs/concepts/calls-and-receipts#what-the-enclave-signs) in its own data: 114 bytes (`voynich/receipt/v1`) when `Model.base` is `None`, or 146 bytes (`voynich/receipt/v2`, appending `base.sha256`) when `Model.base` is `Some`. When `Call.callback_program` is not the default address, the transaction passes `[callback_program, ...callback_accounts]` as remaining accounts after the listed accounts.

- **Checks.** The call is `Open` (`CallNotOpen`) and has `price_unit == PriceUnit::Call` (`InvalidCall`). The current slot is at most the deadline slot (`CallDeadlinePassed`). The record is the one the Enclave names, valid and unexpired (`InvalidEnclave`, `InvalidAttestation`), and its measurement is allowed (`MeasurementNotAllowed`). For a `Sealed` call, `open_answer` is empty; for an `Open` call, `open_answer` is non-empty, at most `Config.max_open_answer_bytes`, and hashes to `output_hash` (`InvalidOpenAnswer`). The Ed25519 instruction at the index matches the signature, the enclave's signing key and the `v1` (standalone) or `v2` (adapter with `Model.base.sha256`) receipt message (`InvalidSignature`). The caller, owner, operator and fee recipient are the ones the Call recorded (`InvalidRecipient`). For a price in a mint: the escrow, owner, operator and protocol accounts are associated token accounts of the right wallet and mint (`InvalidTokenAccount`). The escrow holds the price (`InsufficientEscrow`). When `Call.callback_program` is set: remaining accounts equal `[callback_program, ...callback_accounts]`, `callback_program` is executable, and every callback account marked `is_writable` is passed writable (`InvalidCallback`).
- **Effects.** The operator receives `price × operator_share_bps / 10,000`, rounded down. The fee recipient receives `price × protocol_share_bps / 10,000`, rounded down. The owner receives the rest (or, when `model.has_split` is true, `owner_amount` routes into the canonical `Split` PDA passed in remaining accounts, crediting each recipient's `accrued` share with rounding dust to `recipients[0]` and emitting `SplitCredited`, coexisting with any `callback_program` remaining accounts). The Receipt holds the call, model, enclave, both hashes, the signature, `answer_mode`, `open_answer`, `caller_is_program`, the slot and the three payments. The Call's status is `Settled`, and the model's and the enclave's `calls_settled` rise by one. Emits `CallSettled`. When `Call.callback_program` is set, the program flushes `Receipt`, `Call`, `Model` and `Enclave`, and invokes `callback_program::on_receipt` (discriminator `93db513a4577e15d`) with accounts `[receipt, call, model, ...callback_accounts]` before transferring the escrow. For a price in a mint, the escrow token account is closed and its rent returns to the caller.

## settle_metered_call

Settles a per-token (`PriceUnit::Token`) call at a signed token count, writes a 423-byte metered Receipt, splits `count * call.price` across owner, operator and protocol, and returns the unspent escrow ceiling (`call.ceiling - total`) to `caller` in the same instruction.

<Rows data="ix-settle-metered-call-args" />

<Rows data="ix-settle-metered-call" />

The transaction carries an Ed25519 program instruction at `ed25519_instruction_index` verifying `signature` against `enclave.signing_key` over the [piece message](/docs/concepts/calls-and-receipts#signed-pieces): 120 bytes (`voynich/piece/v1`) when `Model.base` is `None`, or 152 bytes (`voynich/piece/v2`, appending `base.sha256`) when `Model.base` is `Some`. `payer` must be either `call.operator` (the enclave runtime) or `call.caller` (caller self-settlement), preventing third-party observers from front-running a live stream.

- **Checks.** `payer` equals `call.operator` or `call.caller` (`Unauthorized`). The call is `Open` (`CallNotOpen`), has `price_unit == PriceUnit::Token` (`InvalidCall`), and has `answer_mode == AnswerMode::Sealed` (`InvalidOpenAnswer`). The current slot is at most `call.deadline_slot` (`CallDeadlinePassed`). The attestation record is valid, unexpired and matches the Enclave (`InvalidEnclave`, `InvalidAttestation`), and its measurement is allowed (`MeasurementNotAllowed`). `total = count * call.price` does not overflow (`ArithmeticOverflow`) and satisfies `total <= call.ceiling` (`InsufficientEscrow`); `count == 0` is valid and returns 100% of `call.ceiling`. The Ed25519 instruction verifies `signature` over the `voynich/piece/v1` or `v2` message (`InvalidSignature`). The canonical `["receipt", call]` PDA is empty and System-owned (`InvalidCall`). Recipients and optional SPL token accounts match the Call (`InvalidRecipient`, `InvalidTokenAccount`), and any registered `callback_program` remaining accounts pass the same `InvalidCallback` checks as `settle_call`.
- **Effects.** Initializes the canonical `["receipt", call]` PDA at `Receipt::METERED_SPACE` (`423` bytes, handling any pre-funded lamports cleanly via transfer + `allocate` + `assign`) with the 407-byte `Receipt` prefix (`output_hash = running_hash`, `total = count * call.price`) plus trailing `delivered_count: u64` and `returned_amount: u64`. Marks `call.status = Settled`, increments `model.calls_settled` and `enclave.calls_settled`, emits `CallSettled` with `delivered_count = Some(count)` and `returned_amount = Some(returned_amount)`, invokes any registered `on_receipt` callback CPI, and in the same transaction pays `owner_amount`, `operator_amount` and `protocol_amount` while returning `returned_amount` to `caller` (for SOL directly in lamports; for SPL transferring `escrow.amount - total` to `caller_tokens` and closing `escrow_tokens` back to `caller`).

## refund_call

Returns the escrow of a call that did not settle by its deadline. Anyone can send it; the escrow goes back to `caller` (whether a wallet or a calling program's PDA).

<Rows data="ix-refund-call" />

- **Checks.** The call is `Open` (`CallNotOpen`). The current slot is past the deadline slot (`CallNotRefundable`). The caller account is the Call's caller (`InvalidRecipient`). For a price in a mint: the escrow is the Call's associated token account and the destination is a token account of the caller's for the mint (`InvalidTokenAccount`). If a remaining account is passed for operator bond slashing, it must be the canonical writable `Bond` PDA (`["bond", call.enclave]`) owned by the Voynich program (`InvalidBond`).
- **Effects.** The full escrowed amount (`Call::escrow_amount()`: `price` for `Call`, `ceiling` for `Token`) returns to `caller`. The Call's status is `Refunded`. For a price in a mint, the escrow token account is closed and its rent returns to `caller`. When the canonical `Bond` PDA is passed in remaining accounts and holds `bonded_lamports > 0`, the program slashes `min(bonded_lamports, escrow * slash_bps / 10_000)` for SOL calls (or `min(bonded_lamports, 1_000_000)` lamports for SPL calls) directly to `caller`, updates `bonded_lamports`, `slashed_total` and `missed_calls`, and emits `BondSlashed`. Emits `CallRefunded` with `amount` set to the refunded escrow.
